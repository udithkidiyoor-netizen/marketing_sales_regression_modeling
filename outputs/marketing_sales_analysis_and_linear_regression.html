7. Conclusion

This project explored the relationship between marketing expenditure and sales performance using exploratory data analysis and simple linear regression.

After cleaning the dataset and analyzing variable relationships, Radio promotion budget was selected as the predictor variable for modeling sales.

The regression model achieved an R² score of 0.757, indicating that approximately 75.7% of the variation in sales can be explained by radio promotion spending.

The fitted regression equation was:

[ Sales = 8.17(Radio) + 41.53 ]

Model interpretation suggests that for every additional one million invested in radio promotion, predicted sales increase by approximately 8.17 million on average.

Diagnostic evaluation using residual distribution, Q–Q plots, and residual vs fitted analysis indicated that the regression assumptions were reasonably satisfied.

Overall, the analysis demonstrates that radio advertising expenditure shows a strong positive relationship with sales and can serve as an effective predictor of sales performance within this dataset.
